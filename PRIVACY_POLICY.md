# Privacy Policy for WaLiCode

**Last updated: June 8, 2026**

## Information We Collect

WaLiCode is a local-first AI programming IDE. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect personal identification information
- We do not collect device identifiers
- We do not collect usage analytics or tracking data
- We do not collect location data

### Information Processed Locally
- **Source code and project files**: All code you write and edit stays on your device. We never transmit your source code to our servers.
- **AI conversations**: When you use AI features, your prompts and code context are sent directly to the AI provider you configure (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek). These providers have their own privacy policies. We do not intercept or store this data.
- **Git credentials**: Stored locally on your device using the system credential manager. We never transmit your Git credentials.

### Third-Party Services
WaLiCode integrates with the following third-party services based on your configuration:
- **AI Providers** (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, etc.): You provide your own API keys. Prompts and code context are sent directly from your device to the provider's API.
- **Git remote hosts** (GitHub, GitLab, etc.): Standard Git operations via HTTPS or SSH.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. Please review their respective privacy policies.

## Data Storage
All data is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain any cloud servers or databases that store user data.

## Children's Privacy
WaLiCode is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
